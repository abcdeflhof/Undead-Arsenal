Welcome to Undead Arsenal, my second ever programming/game design project!

Quick note: to play, download each file into a folder and then open the "_game2.exe" file.

  Undead Arsenal is my way of gaining more experience with Pygame, the general game design process, and pixel art. Because I'm working with a group of friends as playtesters, it has also required me to take an iterative approach to design. It's much more in-depth than my first project, A Bit Spacey, and has involved much more experimentation in all facets of its creation.
  It has so far taken 4 months of on-and-off work to get Undead Arsenal to its current version of 0.4.1. This includes the time I've spent learning fundamental ideas, like how to have an object point towards the mouse or how to have separate files for different object types.

I created all of the pixel art and code, and made all of the design decisions with help from the feedback of my friends. The font, Ithaca, was made by GGBotNet and I found all of the audio on Pixabay (it's all royalty-free or copyright-free).

The controls for the game are as follows:
[WASD] to move,
[M1] to shoot,
[M2] to break your current gun,
and other prompts will show up while you play! Good luck.
